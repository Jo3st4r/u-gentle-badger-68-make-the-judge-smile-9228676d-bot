## The line

> A stray dog followed me all the way home tonight, then stopped at my gate and waited until my light came on — as if it was the one making sure I got in safe.

Why it lands:

- **Warmth:** a small kindness that is genuinely felt, not
  reported — a stray choosing to walk someone home.
- **Surprise:** the roles flip at the end. The dog is not the
  one being looked after; it is the one making sure I got in
  safe.
- **Concrete detail:** one real small moment — the gate, and
  the light coming on — rather than a general sentiment.
- **Fresh wording:** no greeting-card phrasing, no borrowed
  sentiment; its own rhythm, with the turn landing on the
  em dash.

Not the field's crowded angles (bus fares, fruit on
doorsteps, umbrellas, warm bread) — a guardian stray, and a
light coming on.
